Stop cleaning your shoes on those escalator brushes, because you're actually shoving your foot straight into the most dangerous spot on the whole machine.
Almost everyone thinks they're a free shoe shine. In reality, they're a silent alarm.
See, an escalator's metal steps are constantly moving, while the side wall next to them stays completely still.
That leaves a razor-thin gap right along the edge, and if a shoelace, baggy pant leg, or soft sneaker slips in, the gears don't stop.
That's why safety codes require skirt deflectors.
The second those stiff bristles touch your ankle, your brain flinches and pulls your foot away.
So, next time you go in for a free shine, remember. The escalator isn't cleaning your shoes. It's warning you to move.
